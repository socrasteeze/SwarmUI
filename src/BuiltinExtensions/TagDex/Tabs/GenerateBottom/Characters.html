No Character Data

This picker reads the booru tag vocabulary that Anima, IllustriousXL and NoobAI were trained on. Pick a dataset below to download: 244,932 characters and 419,789 artists, from the Laxhar/noob-wiki dataset on HuggingFace.

Nothing is downloaded until you click, and you can reopen this list any time with the Datasets button above. You can also drop your own CSV into Data/TagDex/ using the same column names.
